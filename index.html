Autonomous Decision Security · v0.2.1 Controlled Pre-Standard

The lifecycle framework for securing autonomous decisions.

Traditional security secures data, models, and infrastructure. ADS-Ω secures the decision — the determination an autonomous system makes — across its full lifecycle, from context acquisition to immutable post-decision audit.

Core Principle

Every autonomous decision SHALL be traceable, explainable, governed, and contained.

The four pillars are operationalized through a seven-layer control plane spanning five lifecycle stages — from context acquisition through post-decision audit.

PILLAR 1
Traceable
Every decision produces a cryptographically verifiable provenance record.
PILLAR 2
Explainable
Reasoning artifacts are recorded with sufficient detail for retrospective analysis.
PILLAR 3
Governed
Execution is bounded by policy, authorization states, and risk thresholds.
PILLAR 4
Contained
Drift, anomaly, and deviation trigger automatic containment activation.
Architecture

Seven layers. One control plane.

Each consequential decision traverses the seven-layer ADS-Ω control plane. Four layers form the Decision Trust Boundary — no execution unless every gate returns PASS.
